## Feature delivered

Admin can now generate a read-only movement trace for one student over a selected date range.

The report includes student identity, date range, movement count, completed outing count, total valid duration, outing type, purpose/location, exit and return times, duration, status and the interpretation disclaimer.

No movement record is treated only as absence of a recorded eOuting movement; it is not proof of physical presence at the institute.
